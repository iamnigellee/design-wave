Dusk: a meditation app for urban professionals who can't sleep. It refuses wellness clichés — no treatment language, no cheerful affirmations. The brand sits with the user at 2 a.m. instead of promising relief. iPhone portrait single-hand is the primary surface; dark is the primary mode (not a variant); restraint is the dominant texture. One signal color (warm flax #A89680) appears only when the UI needs attention — never as decoration.
